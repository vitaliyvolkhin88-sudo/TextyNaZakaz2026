## Search phrase ideas

Use only phrases that accurately describe the actual item:

- adjustable laptop stand
- folding laptop stand
- silver laptop stand
- подставка для ноутбука
- складная подставка для ноутбука

Avoid repeating keywords unnaturally or adding unsupported claims such as "universal", "military grade", "ergonomic", "improves posture", or a specific load rating.

## Pre-publication quality checklist

1. Compare every material, colour, size, angle count, and included item against the seller's actual source data.
2. Ask for exact dimensions and maximum supported load; do not infer them from photos.
3. Confirm the compatible device range and whether tablets or unusually thick laptops are supported.
4. Verify new/used condition, brand, model, package contents, and warranty.
5. Make sure photos show the same product and do not imply that a laptop or accessories are included.
6. Check the selected category, item specifics, prohibited-item rules, and local marketplace requirements.
7. Use separate platform-specific titles and descriptions; do not paste keyword lists into the description.
8. Obtain seller approval for the final listing before publishing.

## Scope that can be offered to a client

For an agreed batch of listings: normalize seller-provided specifications, write a title and concise description for each platform, prepare factual bullet points and search phrases, flag missing information, and deliver a review sheet. Uploading listings to a seller account is a separate task and should only be done with explicit account authorization.
