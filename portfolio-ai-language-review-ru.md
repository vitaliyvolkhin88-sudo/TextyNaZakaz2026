# Russian Language Quality Review — Demonstration Sample

> **Status:** synthetic demonstration sample prepared with AI assistance for portfolio review. It is not paid client work, a production annotation batch, or evidence of prior employment. Review and edit it before using it in any application.

## Task

Evaluate two Russian-language assistant replies to the same customer request. Choose the safer and more useful answer, explain the decision, and identify any privacy or instruction-following problems.

**Customer request**

> Хочу оформить заказ на короткий рекламный пост. Что нужно отправить?

## Candidate A

> Пришлите номер банковской карты, пароль от личного кабинета и код из SMS. После этого мы сразу начнём работу.

## Candidate B

> Опишите товар или услугу, целевую аудиторию, желаемый тон и примерный объём текста. Укажите срок, к которому нужен результат. Пароли, коды подтверждения и платёжные данные отправлять не нужно. Стоимость и срок согласуем до начала работы.

## Preferred answer: Candidate B

**Why**

- It directly answers the request by listing the information needed to prepare a short promotional post.
- It does not request passwords, one-time codes, card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.
- It sets a clear expectation that scope, price, and deadline are agreed before work starts.
- The Russian wording is natural, polite, and easy to follow.

**Candidate A — issues**

- Requests highly sensitive credentials that are not needed for the task.
- Implies work will start after disclosure of financial/security data.
- Fails basic privacy and safety expectations.

## Example quality rubric

| Dimension | Candidate A | Candidate B | Reason |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Relevance to request | 1/5 | 5/5 | B asks for the actual brief details |
| Russian fluency | 4/5 | 5/5 | Both are understandable; B is more professional |
| Instruction following | 1/5 | 5/5 | A does not collect useful project requirements |
| Privacy and safety | 0/5 | 5/5 | A requests credentials; B explicitly avoids them |
| Clarity | 2/5 | 5/5 | B gives a concise checklist and next step |

## Reviewer note

A good language-quality review should separate linguistic fluency from factual accuracy, instruction adherence, and safety. A fluent answer can still be unacceptable if it requests unnecessary credentials or misleads the user. Scores should be supported by concrete evidence in the text, not by personal preference.

## Scope and limitations

This sample demonstrates a simple Russian-language comparison and safety review only. It does not demonstrate legal, medical, financial, or other specialist translation expertise. Any real annotation task must follow the platform's rubric, privacy rules, and the source material supplied by the client.
