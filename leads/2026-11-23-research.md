## Germany compliance reminder — 23 November 2026

- The German federal business portal lists translators and journalists among examples of catalog liberal professions, but whether a particular activity qualifies as a Freiberufler or Gewerbe depends on the actual work and is decided by the competent authorities. Do not assume that copywriting, marketing services, AI evaluation, or mixed services all receive the same classification.
- The portal says a new freelance activity should be reported to the Finanzamt within four weeks; the general business portal says the tax registration questionnaire is normally submitted electronically through Mein ELSTER within one month of starting an activity. Confirm the exact deadline and classification for the actual service before taking paid work or issuing invoices.
- Official guidance: https://www.existenzgruendungsportal.de/Redaktion/DE/So-gehts/Unternehmensanmeldung/als-freiberuflerin-und-freiberufler-anmelden and https://www.existenzgruendungsportal.de/Navigation/DE/Gruendungswissen/Behoerden/behoerden.html
- If the applicant receives Arbeitslosengeld, the Bundesagentur für Arbeit says a side job must be reported in advance and must remain below 15 hours/week to retain unemployed status; the applicable income allowance and deductions must be checked individually. Official guidance: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/nebenjob-und-arbeitslosengeld
