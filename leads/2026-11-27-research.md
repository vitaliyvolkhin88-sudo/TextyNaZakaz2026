Freelancer.com officially recommends Milestone Payments; funded milestones provide access to its dispute-resolution process. Keep messages and payments on the platform. For users with Germany as the verified account country, Freelancer lists Germany among EU Express Withdrawal countries. Availability still depends on the verified account and current withdrawal page.
